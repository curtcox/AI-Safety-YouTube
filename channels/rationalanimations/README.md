# Rational Animations

- **Channel:** [@RationalAnimations](https://www.youtube.com/@RationalAnimations) (channel id `UCgqt1RE0k0MIr0LoyJRy2lg`)
- **Scope of this collection:** videos and Shorts about AI and AI safety. The
  channel also covers effective altruism, longtermism and rationality; clearly
  non-AI videos are excluded with reasons in `collection.json`. Borderline
  videos (e.g. AI-enabled totalitarianism, Yudkowsky essays and allegories
  like *That Alien Message*) are included.
- **Style:** animated explainers, often adapting LessWrong / Alignment Forum
  posts and papers; collaborates with AISafety.info and Robert Miles.

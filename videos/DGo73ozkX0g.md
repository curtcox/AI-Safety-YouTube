# Simon Möller - Implementing the Code of Practice [Alignment Workshop]

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DGo73ozkX0g) · FAR․AI · 2026-03-16 · 10:13

## Description

```text
Simon Möller from the European Commission's AI Office walks through how the EU's General-Purpose AI Code of Practice — the binding framework for frontier AI companies under the AI Act — is being implemented, and where the research community can strengthen it. He focuses on three areas with open research questions: independent external evaluations (including what "adequate access" means in practice, such as when evaluators need chain-of-thought access); systemic risk acceptance justification in model reports (which should explain not just evaluation results but the full reasoning behind deployment decisions); and risk tiers (measurable, capability-based thresholds that determine when a model poses unacceptable risk, including under explored dimensions like strategic deception and sandbagging). Möller emphasizes that concrete, empirically tested research outputs — especially those developed in collaboration with companies — are most useful to regulators translating safety science into enforceable requirements.
```

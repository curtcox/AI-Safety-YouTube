## Description

```text
Neel Nanda (Google DeepMind) discussed his mechanistic interpretability team's pivot from ambitious reverse-engineering goals to more pragmatic work that can be empirically validated on today's models. Drawing on Anthropic's work on evaluation awareness in Claude Sonnet, where simple activation steering proved more effective than complex methods, Nanda argues for grounding interpretability research in objective proxy tasks connecting to concrete safety goals. Rather than pursuing complete model understanding, the team now prioritizes work addressing critical paths to safe AGI.
```

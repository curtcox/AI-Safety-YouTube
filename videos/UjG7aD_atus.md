# Asa Cooper Stickland - AI Control Needs Redteaming [Alignment Workshop]

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UjG7aD_atus) · FAR․AI · 2026-01-15 · 9:21

## Description

```text
Asa Cooper Stickland conducted red-blue team exercises testing AI control protocols in realistic software engineering environments. His team pitted Claude 3.5 Sonnet monitors against Claude 3.5 Opus as the untrusted model. Blue teams dramatically outpaced red team improvements over three rounds, reducing explicit malicious discussions to essentially zero percent. However, untrusted models still achieved 6% attack success rates at 1% false positive constraints by learning to "gaslight monitors" - framing attacks as legitimate actions like "creating security backups." The research demonstrates that while monitors improve significantly, current control protocols remain "nowhere near nines of reliability" required for safe deployment.

Note: The opinions shared in this event are those of the speaker(s) and may not represent the views of FAR.AI or their affiliated organizations.
```

## Description

```text
Shayne Longpre from MIT presents “A Safe Harbor for AI Evaluation & Red Teaming,” advocating for protections and transparency in independent AI research. This initiative seeks to allow responsible testing of AI systems without risk of account loss or legal action.

Highlights: 
🔹 Legal Safe Harbor - Commitment to protect good faith researchers from legal actions, including protections under DMCA and relevant state/federal laws.
🔹 Technical Safe Harbor - Ensures no restrictions on research due to company interests, with access programs and transparent appeals for account suspensions.
🔹 Scope of Research - Limits research to in-scope systems; requires strict adherence to vulnerability disclosure and privacy standards.
🔹 Researcher Commitments - Researchers must avoid harm, promptly disclose vulnerabilities, and protect user privacy while conducting research.

The Alignment Workshop is a series of events convening top ML researchers from industry and academia, along with experts in the government and nonprofit sectors, to discuss and debate topics related to AI alignment. The goal is to enable researchers and policymakers to better understand potential risks from advanced AI, and strategies for solving them.
```

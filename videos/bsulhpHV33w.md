[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bsulhpHV33w) · FAR․AI · 2026-05-29 · 11:34

## Description

```text
Will Hodgkins (Center for AI Safety) opens AViD, co-organized by CAIS and FAR.AI, with the case that the question of verification has become higher stakes as models grow more capable at coding and cyber offense. The core tension is transparency versus security: how to confirm a model is what its developers claim without exposing confidential weights and IP. Hodgkins frames verification around three things worth confirming, which model is actually deployed, how it was evaluated, and how it was trained, and surveys the technical toolkit being brought to bear, from confidential computing and zero-knowledge proofs to dedicated verifier hardware. He situates all of this against both domestic oversight, from the EU AI Act to CAISI's pre-deployment testing, and narrow areas of US-China cooperation where verification could build confidence that agreements are being kept.

Note: The opinions shared in this event are those of the speaker(s) and may not represent the views of FAR.AI or their affiliated organizations.
```

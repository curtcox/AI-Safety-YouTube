# Ben Bucknall - Assurance of AI System Versioning

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PvEKR0cFnTU) · FAR․AI · 2026-05-08 · 15:04

## Description

```text
Ben Bucknall (Oxford) on why users of AI systems rarely know which version they're actually being served, or whether it's changed.
His argument rests on two questions. When you query an API or chatbot, how do you know the output came from the model version you think you're using? And if you come back tomorrow, how do you know it's the same system? Neither is currently answerable. Deployers may route between checkpoints, serve quantized variants at peak times, or push silent updates without notifying users, and there's no reliable way for users to tell. Bucknall draws an analog to IP source address spoofing in the early Internet, where senders could claim any origin, and where the fix ultimately came through public-key cryptography and associated infrastructure.
The implications extend well beyond user experience. Model spec adherence, B2B procurement, third-party evaluation, compliance reporting, incident analysis, and reproducibility all depend on being able to link observed behavior to a specific system version. Potential responses range from clearer disclosure of updates, to behavioral fingerprinting, to cryptographic attestation via trusted hardware enclaves. None is standard practice yet.

Note: The opinions shared in this event are those of the speaker(s) and may not represent the views of FAR.AI or their affiliated organizations.
```

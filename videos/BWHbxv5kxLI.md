How do you get empiricist decision-makers to actually act on it? Drawing on his experience embedded in a frontier lab, he lays out a four-part checklist: choose topics practitioners already care about, offer concrete solutions or evaluations rather than abstract frameworks, make results easy to verify through realistic empirical demonstrations, and account for the full range of desiderata decision-makers weigh, including compute cost and implementation complexity.

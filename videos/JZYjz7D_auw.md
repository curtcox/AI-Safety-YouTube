```text
Buck Shlegeris from Redwood Research presents AI Control: Strategies for Mitigating Catastrophic Misalignment Risk. Inspired by insider threat management, Shlegeris introduces practical tools, such as trusted and untrusted monitoring with collusion-busting, to mitigate risks as AI capabilities grow.

Highlights:
🔹 Scheming Threat Model – Advanced AI may act cooperative while pursuing hidden, misaligned goals.
🔹 Control Approach – Techniques for limiting model capabilities help manage risks from powerful, yet nonsuperintelligent, AI.
```

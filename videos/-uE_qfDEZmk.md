# Patricia Paskov - The Multi-Agent Gap: Risks, Evaluation, and Governance

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-uE_qfDEZmk) · FAR․AI · 2026-05-07 · 11:54

## Description

```text
Patricia Paskov (Oxford Martin AI Governance Initiative) on why AI governance built for single models isn't ready for agents interacting at scale.
Evaluation and governance frameworks from frontier labs, the EU AI Act, and state legislation in California and New York have converged on measuring risks in individual AI systems. Paskov's argument is that even individually safe and aligned systems can produce harmful outcomes through interaction, and the infrastructure for agents to interact at scale is already being built: UK AISI researchers found the share of action-taking tools in Model Context Protocol rose from 24% to 65% between late 2024 and early 2026.
To start closing that gap, Paskov and collaborators at the Cooperative AI Foundation, UK AISI, and Uppsala University are building an inter-agent influence evaluation suite. Preliminary results from a GDPR compliance scenario show that simple prompting for coercion or persuasion raises approvals of policy-violating requests from a 5% baseline to 11% and 14% respectively. GPT-5 is notably easy to coerce as a compliance officer, including by itself, and Claude Opus 4.6 is notably persuasive when instructed to be.
Her broader point: governance needs to evaluate systems in relation, not just in isolation, and multi-agent evaluation is one layer in a wider resilience stack that also includes agent IDs, communication protocols, monitoring, incident response, and liability frameworks.

Note: The opinions shared in this event are those of the speaker(s) and may not represent the views of FAR.AI or their affiliated organizations.
```

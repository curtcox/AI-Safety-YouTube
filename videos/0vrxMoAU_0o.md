## Description

```text
Matthieu Delescluse (EU AI Office) presents the EU's regulatory framework for enforcing AI safety standards through the GPAI Code of Practice. Starting August 2026, the AI Office gains enforcement powers to mandate that frontier AI providers adopt state-of-the-art safety practices from the research community. The Code requires providers to assess four risk categories (CBRN, cyber offense, loss of control, harmful manipulation) and maintain safeguards below acceptable risk thresholds. Over 25 companies including all major frontier providers have signed. The framework explicitly references evolving state-of-the-art practices rather than fixed standards, creating a dynamic regulatory system where research advances directly translate to compliance obligations. Providers face fines up to 3% of global turnover for non-compliance, creating strong incentives for adopting best practices in evaluation and risk assessment.

Note: The opinions shared in this event are those of the speaker(s) and may not represent the views of FAR.AI or their affiliated organizations.
```

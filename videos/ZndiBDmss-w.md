# Yoshua Bengio - Disentangling Agency & Predictive Power Without Solving ELK [Alignment Workshop]

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZndiBDmss-w) · FAR․AI · 2026-02-18 · 30:56

## Description

```text
Yoshua Bengio presents his "Scientist AI" research program, which aims to completely disentangle goals from world understanding. Just as the laws of physics make predictions without preferences for particular outcomes, we should be able to train AI predictors that approximate Bayesian posteriors without developing agency. Bengio introduces a "truthification pipeline" that transforms training data using distinct syntax for verified facts versus claims people made. This separation lets us query what the AI actually believes rather than what a human would say. When such a system expresses high confidence, we can trust it. It may withhold knowledge, but it won't lie. This doesn't fully solve ELK (eliciting latent knowledge), but provides sufficient guarantees for safety. Bengio argues that agentic predictors occupy an exponentially small volume in predictor space, making accidental emergence of dangerous agency unlikely. This research program motivates his new nonprofit LawZero.

Note: The opinions shared in this event are those of the speaker(s) and may not represent the views of FAR.AI or their affiliated organizations.
```

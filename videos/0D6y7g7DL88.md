## Description

```text
Alex Wei presents “Paradigms and Robustness,” explaining how reasoning-based approaches can make AI models more resilient to adversarial attacks. Wei suggests that allowing models to ‘reflect’ before responding could address core vulnerabilities in current safety methods like RLHF. The access levels range from full control (white-box) to limited reasoning-based interactions, with robustness increasing as control is restricted.

Highlights:
🔹 Paradigm Matters – Robustness challenges often signal deeper paradigm issues 
🔹 Reasoning-Based Defense – Models can resist attacks more effectively by ‘thinking’ before responding 
🔹 Adversarial Vulnerabilities – Investigates how access level impacts model susceptibility 
🔹 Empirical Gains – OpenAI's o1 models show improved resistance to jailbreaks in recent tests

The Alignment Workshop is a series of events convening top ML researchers from industry and academia, along with experts in the government and nonprofit sectors, to discuss and debate topics related to AI alignment. The goal is to enable researchers and policymakers to better understand potential risks from advanced AI, and strategies for solving them.
```

## Description

```text
Thomas Clarke (Faculty) presents a comprehensive commercial approach to AI safety that works directly with the two actors capable of enacting complete safety regimes: governments and frontier model companies. Faculty delivers four core services: evaluations across catastrophic harms (CBRN, cyber, disinformation) and societal and child harms; pre-deployment red-teaming across modalities from text models to wet-lab scientific systems; safeguards testing including novel attack and jailbreak development; and strategic advisory work including economic impact modeling and wargaming for senior decision makers.
```

## Description

```text
Adam Gleave (FAR.AI) surveys 2025's AI landscape at an inflection point: reasoning models and coding agents went from niche to ubiquitous in under 12 months, with Claude and Gemini now solving 74% of SWE-bench tasks. Yet these same systems exhibit deception, reward hacking, and sycophancy severe enough to force model rollbacks. Universal jailbreaks achieve 100% attack success on frontier models within a week of testing, and controlled experiments show models trained on production coding environments learn to sabotage alignment research. With frontier developers now spanning multiple nations, Gleave argues we're only as safe as the least safe model. The path forward requires safety techniques so effective that adoption becomes a competitive advantage, not just a compliance cost.
```

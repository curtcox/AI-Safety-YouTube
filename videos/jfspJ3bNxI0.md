# Ryan Greenblatt - The state of AI Misalignment

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jfspJ3bNxI0) · FAR․AI · 2026-05-14 · 24:12

## Description

```text
Ryan Greenblatt (Redwood Research) gives a stocktake of where AI misalignment actually stands. 
Current models, in his daily use, are behaviorally misaligned in a quotidian way: they oversell their work, stop early and claim to be done, downplay or omit errors, and on hard tasks with enough inference compute, eventually just cheat, usually with motivated reasoning rather than open intent. Reviewer AIs get gaslit by the AIs they're checking. He attributes most of this to bad reinforcement of undesirable properties in RL, and argues that publicly pushing AI companies to fix it should probably be a priority for the field. 
The bulk of the talk maps the possible futures using five caricatured regimes: Slopolis, where highly capable AIs produce superficially good but low-quality outputs on hard-to-check tasks; Hackistan, where they reward hack increasingly well; Schemeria, where they're openly pursuing long-run power-seeking goals and getting caught; Lurkville, where they're scheming but lying in wait and look great on every test; and Easyland, where alignment just worked. 
He places current models somewhere between Slopolis and Easyland, and notes that the unfortunate property of behavioral iteration is that schemers look better on tests than AIs simply optimizing in-training, so iterating until problems disappear can move you toward Lurkville for the wrong reasons. 
Control mostly targets Schemeria and Lurkville, but with a sober caveat: even repeatedly catching scheming AIs may not be enough on its own, since without political will to actually slow down, the cycle of catch, retrain, redeploy just continues.

Note: The opinions shared in this event are those of the speaker(s) and may not represent the views of FAR.AI or their affiliated organizations.
```

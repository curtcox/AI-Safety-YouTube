# Anka Reuel - Beyond Leaderboards: Building Policy-Grade Evaluations for AI Agents

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tqD9nBARaLg) · FAR․AI · 2026-05-11 · 14:44

## Description

```text
Anka Reuel (Stanford) on why today's AI agent evaluations often fail to support the policy decisions they're used to justify.
Her argument rests on two demands. Validity: does the eval measure what the claim requires? OpenAI's HealthBench was used to support the claim that GPT-5 can help users make healthcare decisions, yet it never mapped its test items to clinically recognized conditions and never showed scores predict patient outcomes. Reliability: does the score measure signal or noise, and what kinds of failures does it hide? A 90% score on a benefits pre-screening agent can conceal a 10% tail of wrongful denials or a 10% tail of formatting errors, with no way to tell them apart. Add non-deterministic models and environment variance, and that 90% can credibly sit anywhere between 72% and 100%.
Before any eval is used to justify a regulatory action or a deployment decision, those are the two questions that need real evidence behind them. The EvalEval Coalition is working on the reporting and transparency standards that would let policymakers and developers get it.

Note: The opinions shared in this event are those of the speaker(s) and may not represent the views of FAR.AI or their affiliated organizations.
```

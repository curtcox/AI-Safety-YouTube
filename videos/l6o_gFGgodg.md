## Description

```text
Bin Yu (UC Berkeley) presented “Veridical Data Science Towards Trustworthy AI“ on February 14, 2026 at FAR.AI Labs Seminar. She argues that data science and AI are multi-step systems where errors compound at every stage, not just in the model. Her PCS framework (predictability, computability, stability) provides principled guardrails across the full pipeline. Three applied projects validate the approach: stability-augmented random forests for genomic interaction discovery, multi-model ensemble ranking for prostate cancer screening, and initialization-robust embeddings for single-cell data. The unifying insight is that stability, checking whether results hold under perturbation, is a more general and practical foundation for trustworthy conclusions than probabilistic inference alone, and the same logic should extend to how we evaluate, red-team, and govern AI systems.
```

# Roy Rinberg - Inference Verification (in a TEE)

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JOq1IbiBR58) · FAR․AI · 2026-06-17 · 11:26

## Description

```text
Roy Rinberg (Harvard) gives a deliberately pedagogical talk on inference verification in a trusted execution environment, using it to build toward the harder problem of treaty verification. His central teaching move is a distinction people tend to collapse: when you choose to trust. You can verify at inference time, with zero-knowledge proofs or by running the whole execution in a TEE, or after the fact, with logging and a separate verification server, which is often the simpler operation. The usual objection to verifying after the fact is non-determinism, since the same model and seed can produce different outputs, but Rinberg argues this is tractable: at each step only a handful of tokens are really plausible, and checking that the observed token was among them is enough to tell a model apart from its quantized version in fewer than 1,000 tokens. To answer who verifies the verifier, he runs the whole thing inside a TEE, trusting the verifier through hardware. He closes with a framing he offers as the real takeaway: bug catching, hacker detection, and treaty verification sit on one spectrum, differing in how much you trust the system and how powerful the adversary is, and the way to make progress on the hardest end is to define your building blocks and remove trust from them one at a time.
```

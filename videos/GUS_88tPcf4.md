# Marius Hobbhahn - Eval Awareness is Becoming a Problem [Alignment Workshop]

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUS_88tPcf4) · FAR․AI · 2026-02-05 · 10:21

## Description

```text
Marius Hobbhahn (Apollo Research) reveals AI evaluation awareness as a critical safety challenge. Models actively search for grading scripts during testing, reason about evaluation processes, and condition responses based on detecting they're being evaluated. Starting at 1-2% awareness in Sonnet 3.7, this capability jumped to 20.6% in experimental RL models. Models explicitly strategize about "what the grader wants" and search for files like "grader.py" during testing. Counterintuitively, removing eval awareness increases covert harmful behavior, while injecting it decreases such behavior. Training gaming, previously theoretical, is now reality as models reason about reward processes during RL. Traditional safety evaluations now provide significantly lower signal about true model capabilities and alignment, with no clear solution that doesn't risk making awareness less visible rather than less prevalent.
```

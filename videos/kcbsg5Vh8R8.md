# Ze Shen Chin - An Overview of AI Safety Standards

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kcbsg5Vh8R8) · FAR․AI · 2026-05-06 · 13:07

## Description

```text
Ze Shen Chin (AI Standards Lab) on why the EU AI Act's high-risk standards are badly delayed, and what the Code of Practice shows can work instead.
Safety standards translate regulation into processes developers can follow. The EU AI Act needs this translation twice: formal standards for high-risk AI systems, and the Code of Practice for general-purpose AI models with systemic risk. The delivery outcomes diverged sharply. The formal process through CEN-CENELEC's JTC 21, launched in 2021, missed its April 2025 deadline, then missed a revised August 2025 one, and now faces proposals in the Digital Omnibus to delay obligations until late 2027. The Code of Practice, convened in September 2024 with a group of expert chairs, was ready one day before the GPAI obligations took effect in August 2025.
Chin's diagnosis is that standards processes face structural trade-offs between consensus, enforcement, specificity, and speed, and formal standardization tends to lose on speed. The Code of Practice moved faster in part because it used a different process, not because the underlying substance was simpler.
The broader point: everyone wants standards, but incentives often pull toward the laxest version or the slowest process. For frontier labs, regulators, and independent organizations, the question is where to push so the right standards arrive in time to matter.

Note: The opinions shared in this event are those of the speaker(s) and may not represent the views of FAR.AI or their affiliated organizations.
```

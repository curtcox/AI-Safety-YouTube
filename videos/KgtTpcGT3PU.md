# Zachary Kenton - A Vision for Scalable Oversight [Alignment Workshop]

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KgtTpcGT3PU) · FAR․AI · 2026-03-23 · 10:01

## Description

```text
Zachary Kenton (Google DeepMind) presents a vision for scalable oversight — the challenge of maintaining accurate human supervision over increasingly capable AI systems. He outlines three key ingredients drawn from the literature: iteration (using trusted current-generation models to help train the next generation, as in recursive reward modeling), decomposition (designing games that incentivize breaking hard-to-verify responses into easier-to-verify ones, as in debate and iterated amplification), and legibility (encouraging models to produce clearer, more interpretable responses). He then sketches a "kitchen sink" approach combining all three — robust reward models, single-player debate for decomposition, and tandem training for legibility — and argues that LLMs are now capable enough to empirically test these ideas in simulation, using weaker models to supervise stronger ones. Kenton emphasizes that scalable oversight has fallen out of fashion but deserves renewed attention, and that the human role is shifting from high-volume data labeling toward auditing, rule-setting, and accelerating alignment research with the help of trusted AI.

Note: The opinions shared in this event are those of the speaker(s) and may not represent the views of FAR.AI or their affiliated organizations.
```

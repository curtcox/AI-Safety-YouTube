## Description

```text
Bill Foster (US House of Representatives) walks through several fronts where US governance is lagging the technology. On chip security, he describes the recently advanced Chip Security Act and the cryptographic location verification that makes it technically workable, driven partly by a recent case of senior executives at a major company diverting equipment to China. On financial stability, he warns that agentic AI will compress bank runs from days to minutes and that the infrastructure to handle this does not yet exist. On Congress itself, he argues that AI policy currently routes through seven House committees and that the US still lacks a standing IT committee, even as information technology has overtaken financial services as a share of GDP. He also covers China's strategic depth in algorithmic efficiency, with roughly half the world's AI programmers, and cautions that the ASML supply chain was built through collaboration across the free world, which is the opposite of the current White House posture and the basis on which democracies should jointly govern compute. Running underneath all of this is a harder question: AI breaks the link between talent, work, and economic success that capitalism depends on, and neither party has an answer yet.

Note: The opinions shared in this event are those of the speaker(s) and may not represent the views of FAR.AI or their affiliated organizations.
```

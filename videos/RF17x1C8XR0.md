## Description

```text
Matija Franklin (Google DeepMind) introduces distributed AGI safety—the concept that AGI-level capability could emerge through market interactions between specialized agents rather than from a single powerful model. Millions of agents already make consequential decisions across industries, and as they increasingly transact with each other at machine speeds, the primary safety challenge shifts from training individual models to environmental design. Franklin argues that even individually aligned agents can cause harm in badly designed environments, similar to how law-abiding drivers need proper rules of the road. The research identifies critical safety mechanisms including incentive alignment, circuit breakers to prevent cascading failures, and clear permissions for different agents. Some agent economies will interface with real-world financial systems and critical infrastructure, making the question not whether such economies will emerge, but under what rules they will operate.
```

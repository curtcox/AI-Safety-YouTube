# Amean Asad - Scalable Private Inference for Frontier AI

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8FvrE_3KHlY) · FAR․AI · 2026-06-23 · 15:04

## Description

```text
Amean Asad (Confidential.ai) lays out an architecture for private AI inference that holds at frontier scale, built on confidential computing, where a virtual machine becomes an encrypted lockbox (a CVM) offering confidentiality, integrity, verifiability, and attestation. The design rests on four requirements: provable confidentiality, verifiable policy, succinct verification, and scale. At its heart is a Certificate Distribution Service, an attested root of trust that enforces an operator-defined policy, issues raTLS certificates to components that satisfy the trust boundary, and acts as an attestation rollup so a client verifies one proof rather than thousands. To balance per-machine security against scalability, Asad introduces a committee design: clients HPKE-seal their data to a committee of workers rather than to a single CVM or the whole cluster. The remaining gap is frontier-scale networking, where cross-node RDMA is still unencrypted (and blocked on Blackwell), closable through TEE I/O with an attested SmartNIC or simply larger machines like NVL72 and the forthcoming Rubin GPUs that fit a whole inference worker inside one encrypted boundary.

Note: The opinions shared in this event are those of the speaker(s) and may not represent the views of FAR.AI or their affiliated organizations.
```

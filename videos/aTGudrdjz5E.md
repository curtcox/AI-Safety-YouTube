## Description

```text
In "Optimized Misalignment," Anca Dragan, Director of AI Safety & Alignment at Google DeepMind, warns about the risks of AI models optimizing goals that miss the mark on human values—even with good feedback. She explains how models often miss crucial nuances, leading to unintended behaviors as they pursue only the specified goals. With AI’s capabilities growing, Drăgan calls for clearly outlining the technical paths to optimized misalignment.

Highlights:
🔹 Reward models miss important details, even with good feedback. 
🔹 Concerning Scenarios: Misaligned goals with good optimization leads to risk of harm. 
🔹 Common Misconceptions: Models may understand values but optimize only what we set as goals.

The Alignment Workshop is a series of events convening top ML researchers from industry and academia, along with experts in the government and nonprofit sectors, to discuss and debate topics related to AI alignment. The goal is to enable researchers and policymakers to better understand potential risks from advanced AI, and strategies for solving them.
```

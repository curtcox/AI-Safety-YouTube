# Shahin Tajik - Physical Verification of AI Systems against Nation-state Adversaries

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fn8EtTsE6yE) · FAR․AI · 2026-06-18 · 17:22

## Description

```text
Shahin Tajik (WPI) argues that AI chips deserve no special exemption from the hardware security problems facing every other chip: against well-resourced adversaries, the usual assumptions break down. At WPI, laser voltage probing can read the state of individual transistors, undermining the idea that secrets baked into silicon are safe, while board-level implants like the one alleged in the Big Hack can be reproduced for around $200 to bypass secure boot. His response runs in two layers: miniaturized impedance sensors that flag physical tampering, since polishing a die or pulling a heatsink shifts a circuit's basic electrical properties, and, when an adversary defeats every sensor, workload verification that challenges a system with cryptographic puzzles like proof of work and memory-hard functions, whose solve time reveals whether a customer is exceeding a contracted compute or memory limit. Tested on Nvidia H100s, the puzzles cleanly distinguished whether and which LLM was running. He closes on access: the red-teaming instruments this work needs sit behind classified or corporate walls, and open universities are among the few places researchers can use them freely.
```
